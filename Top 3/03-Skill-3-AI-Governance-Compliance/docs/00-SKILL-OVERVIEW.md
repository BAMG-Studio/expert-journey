# Skill 3: AI Governance & Compliance Frameworks

## What This Skill Covers
Applying NIST AI RMF, NIST 800-53 Rev 5, FedRAMP continuous monitoring, SBOM
generation, and Responsible AI practices to AI/ML systems.

## Core Frameworks
| Framework | Purpose | Key Focus |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| NIST AI RMF | AI-specific risk management | Govern, Map, Measure, Manage |
| NIST 800-53 Rev 5 | Security/privacy controls | 20 control families |
| FedRAMP | Federal cloud authorization | Continuous monitoring |
| CycloneDX/Syft | SBOM generation for ML | Dependency tracking |
| Responsible AI | Ethics & fairness | Bias, explainability, privacy |

## Why This Matters for Interos
- Government/defense customers require FedRAMP
- Supply chain AI must demonstrate fairness (no geographic bias)
- NIST AI RMF is becoming mandatory for federal AI procurement
- SBOM requirements expanding to include ML model components
- Responsible AI builds customer trust and avoids regulatory penalties

## Learning Path
1. Day 1-2: NIST AI RMF Deep Dive (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage)
2. Day 3-4: NIST 800-53 Controls Mapped to AI Systems
3. Day 5-6: FedRAMP Continuous Monitoring Implementation
4. Day 7-8: SBOM Generation with Syft and CycloneDX
5. Day 9-10: Responsible AI - Fairness, Explainability, Privacy-Preserving ML
6. Day 11-12: Interview Prep + Practice Scenarios
